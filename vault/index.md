## Concepts

Ideas and techniques explained by my sources.

- [[Retrieval-Augmented Generation]] - Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) is a technique that allows large language models (LLMs) to retrieve and incorporate new information from external data sources.
- [[Transformer Architecture]] - The Transformer Architecture has primary components including tokenizers, an embedding layer, transformer layers, and an un-embedding layer.
- [[Transformer Efficient Implementations]] - Techniques that make transformers faster or cheaper to run: KV caching, continuous batching, quantization, FlashAttention, multi-query and latent attention, speculative decoding, and sub-quadratic attention variants.
- [[Transformer Training Methods]] - Transformer training methods cover techniques for stabilizing training, regularization, pretraining and fine-tuning, knowledge distillation, and various language modeling tasks.
